Detailed Description

Recycle bin: the $I index files of \\$Recycle.Bin\\<SID>\\ on every fixed NTFS volume.

WHAT THE ARTEFACT PROVES. A file sent to the recycle bin is renamed $R<random><extension> and Windows writes beside it $I<same> — the original path, the size and the instant of the deletion. Until the bin is emptied, it names who deleted (the SID folder), what and when; emptied, the names of both files remain as deleted records of mft.json.

ONLY THE $I ARE COLLECTED: the $R are the deleted files themselves, of any size; their presence and size are in mft.json, and –binary collects the executables among them.

THE FORMAT (little-endian): 0 8 version: 1 (Vista to 8.1) or 2 (Windows 10 and later) 8 8 size of the deleted file, bytes 16 8 deletion, FILETIME UTC v1: 24 520 original path, UTF-16, 260 characters, zero-padded v2: 24 4 length of the path in characters, terminating zero included 28 … original path, UTF-16
