◆ otherBiasesMinutes

std::vector<long> LocalBias::otherBiasesMinutes

The other offsets whose stretch of the history also holds the time: a local time lived twice — the zone set back (measured, Server 2025 from Paris to New York: 16:45 local lived at 15:45Z, then at 21:45Z) or the hour of autumn. Each its own UTC instant, as likely as the one chosen: said, never dropped. For a time never lived (an hour skipped), the offset after the change, a reading as possible as the one before (otherBiasesAtLocal). Empty when the time was lived once.
