Detailed Description

THE PRIMITIVES OF A DELETED FILE'S RECOVERY (B-036). A deleted file is reached by its $MFT record, not by a path: its record is read as it is now, its clusters checked against $Bitmap, then read — the volume as it is now only, never a shadow copy. Every range is bounded by the volume.

The geometry of an NTFS volume.
