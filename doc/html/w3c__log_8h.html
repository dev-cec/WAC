Detailed Description

Logs in the W3C extended format: the Windows Firewall's (pfirewall.log, firewall_log.h) and IIS's (web_server.h).

THE FORMAT. Text lines; directives first, each starting with "#": "#Version:", "#Software:", "#Date:", "#Fields:" — the names of the fields of the lines that follow, in their order — and, for the firewall only, "#Time Format:". Then one line per record, the fields separated by spaces, "-" for a field without value. A log may repeat its directives further down (IIS writes them again each time it restarts): a new "#Fields:" applies to the lines after it.

THE TIME ZONE OF THE DATES depends on the writer: the firewall writes local time and says so ("#Time Format: Local"); IIS writes UTC and says nothing (W3C's own rule). Each caller gives its default; a header that names another format leaves the date as stored, never converted with a guessed zone.
