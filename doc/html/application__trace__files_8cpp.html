◆ addRecordTimes()

void addRecordTimes ( Json & row,
const WrittenTime & time,
bool writesUtc )

Writes a record's time as every artefact does: Time, in the examined machine's local time with its offset, and TimeUtc. A time with its offset is that instant; one without is read as the examined machine's local time (suspectLocalToUtc, its rules for that date), or as UTC for a product that writes UTC — TimeZoneAssumed says which. A local time written while another zone was in force, as the System log shows it, takes that zone's offset, given in TimeZoneInForce; one written where the zone changed at an unknown instant (a Windows before 10) keeps the current zone's rules, and TimeZoneChangedBetween gives the interval. TimeAsWritten keeps the text. Exactly as many digits of fraction as the line wrote, none invented: an offset or a zone moves whole minutes, never the fraction. Past seven digits (100 ns), the cut is said (TimePrecisionCut). Nothing for a record without a time.

timethe time read at the head of the record
writesUtcthe product writes UTC without saying so

◆ leadingTime()

bool leadingTime ( const std::wstring & line,
WrittenTime & time,
const LineLayout & layout = {} )

Reads the time a log line starts with, after blanks and one [ or <: yyyy-MM-dd, yyyy/MM/dd or yyyy.MM.dd — or MM/dd/yyyy for a product that declares it (LineLayout::monthFirst) —, a space or T, HH:mm:ss, an optional fraction (. or ,, : month first, up to nine digits), an optional offset (Z, ±hh:mm, ±hhmm). Days of the month, hours, minutes and seconds within their range. A date day first, or month first for any other product, is not read: written 08/11/2026, it is two dates. For a product that declares it (LineLayout::processIdFirst), a process identifier and one space may come first: the time is what follows.

◆ textRecords()

std::vector< TextRecord > textRecords ( const std::wstring & text,
const LineLayout & layout = {} )

Cuts a text into records: each line starting with a time opens one, each line without a time that follows a dated record continues it, any other line is a record of its own. Blank lines are left out; "\r\n" and "\n" end a line. A block holding a secret — a private key, an OpenVPN key, an .ovpn's <pkcs12> or <auth-user-pass> — is left out up to its closing line, its opening line kept alone and marked (secretLeftOut).
