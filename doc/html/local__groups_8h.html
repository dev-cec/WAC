Detailed Description

Local groups (aliases) of the examined machine, read from the SAM.

WHAT IT SHOWS. Which accounts hold which rights by membership: who is in Administrators, who in Remote Desktop Users, who in Backup Operators. An attacker who adds an account to a privileged group leaves it here, and the membership is a fact the account list alone does not give.

SOURCE. SAM\Domains\Builtin\Aliases (the built-in groups, S-1-5-32-…) and SAM\Domains\Account\Aliases (the groups created on the machine). Each alias key carries a C value: a 13-DWORD header (data at offset 0x34) with the offset and length of the name, of the description, and of the member list — a packed sequence of binary SIDs. The member SIDs are resolved offline (getNameFromSid), never by asking the system.
