Detailed Description

NTFS volumes: boot sector ($Boot) and change journal header ($UsnJrnl:$Max).

WHAT THE ARTEFACT PROVES.

  • $Boot holds the volume's SERIAL NUMBER, the one shortcuts, jump lists and Prefetch files record of the volume their target was on (VolumeSerial, its low 32 bits): it ties those traces to this disk. Its geometry (sector, cluster and record sizes, place of the $MFT and of its mirror) is what any later reading of the volume needs.
  • $Max is the change journal's header. UsnJournalId is the instant the journal was CREATED: a journal deleted and recreated — fsutil usn deletejournal, an intruder wiping the history of their files — shows a creation far later than the installation. LowestValidUsn says how much of the journal was already overwritten.

Both are read raw, from the copies in the exhibit store; $Boot's first sector is checked by its own signatures ("NTFS ", 0x55AA) before any field is trusted.
