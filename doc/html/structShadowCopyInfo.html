◆ deleted

bool ShadowCopyInfo::deleted = false

Deleted, its store kept: the catalog entry carries flag 0x100. Windows no longer lists it, but keeps its store while an older copy reads through it (Vista, measured: the newest copy, deleted while an older one read through its store, kept its entry flagged 0x140, where the others had 0x40; under 8.1 the oldest copy, deleted, left the catalog). Its store intact, it is read like the others.
