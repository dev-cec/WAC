◆ clockCorrection100ns

long long SystemInfo::clockCorrection100ns = 0

Total of the clock corrections applied since the boot, in 100 ns units (the kernel's BootTimeBias). Positive: the clock was moved forward. A gap of a few seconds is the ordinary adjustment (NTP synchronisation, a VM resuming from suspension); a large gap signals a modified clock — and therefore timestamps to be interpreted with care.
