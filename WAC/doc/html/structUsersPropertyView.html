std::wstring UsersPropertyView::data

Raw content, in hexadecimal, when the signature is not recognised.

MEASURED ON A REAL COLLECTION (2026-09-15): 75 of the 89 USERS_PROPERTY_VIEW carried an unknown signature (0xc1ec0c9) and came out with nothing but their type and that signature as content. The dump existed, but went only into the LOG: at the default log level, 75 objects therefore vanished entirely from the output. See the quality bar: an object that is not decoded returns its bytes.

◆ itemType

std::wstring UsersPropertyView::itemType

Nature of the item, deduced from its signature.

The signature does not only choose a decoder: at libyal (libfwsi) it IDENTIFIES the type. Two of the signatures handled here do not name "users property views" but MTP devices — a volume and a file entry —, that is the trace that a phone or a camera was plugged in and browsed. The generic class name hid that fact.
