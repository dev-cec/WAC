std::wstring serviceSid ( const std::wstring &  service)

The service SID Windows derives from a service name: "S-1-5-80-" followed by the five little-endian 32-bit words of the SHA-1 of the name in upper case, in UTF-16LE. Checked against LookupAccountNameW("NT SERVICE\\<name>") for every service of the test machine (system_conversions_test.cpp).
