std::wstring BinaryFingerprint::authenticodeSha256

Authenticode SHA-256 of a PE: the digest the signature and the catalogs cover. The only digest of an authentic Microsoft binary that –collect fingerprinted without copying it (no MD5, SHA-1, SHA-256 then).
