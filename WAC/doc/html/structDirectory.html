Detailed Description

One entry of the OLE directory: a stream or a storage, found by its name.

Only what WAC uses is read: the name, the first sector and the size. The entry also carries a type, a colour, sibling ids, a CLSID, flags and two dates; they were decoded into members that only an uncalled toJson() read — dead code, whose dates were moreover converted as local times while MS-CFB defines them in UTC.
